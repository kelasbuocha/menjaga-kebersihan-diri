Portal 1

Vocabulary & Habits

Kebiasaan Menjaga Kebersihan Diri Saya

✨
💡 Kebersihan Diri adalah usaha merawat dan membersihkan tubuh agar tetap sehat, nyaman, dan terhindar dari berbagai masalah kesehatan.

Key Sentences (Kalimat Kunci)

"I keep my body clean."

Saya menjaga tubuh saya tetap bersih.

"I have good hygiene habits."

Saya memiliki kebiasaan menjaga kebersihan yang baik.

"I want to stay healthy."

Saya ingin tetap sehat.

6 Kebiasaan Penting (Hygiene Habits)

🛁

1. Take a bath

Mandi

"I take a bath every day." — Mandi membantu membersihkan tubuh dari keringat dan kotoran.

🪥

2. Brush your teeth

Menyikat gigi

"I brush my teeth twice a day." — Menyikat gigi secara teratur membantu menjaga kebersihan gigi dan mulut.

🧴

3. Wash your face

Mencuci wajah

"I wash my face every morning." — Mencuci wajah membantu menghilangkan keringat dan kotoran.

🧴

4. Wash your hair

Mencuci rambut

"I wash my hair three times a week." — Frekuensi mencuci rambut dapat disesuaikan dengan kebutuhan.

🪮

5. Comb your hair

Menyisir rambut

"I comb my hair every morning." — Menyisir rambut membantu merapikan rambut.

💅

6. Clip your nails

Memotong kuku

"I clip my nails once a week." — Memotong kuku secara teratur membantu menjaga kebersihan kuku.

Portal 2

Grammar & Sentence Patterns

Pola Kalimat Bahasa Inggris

1

"You Should ..." (Memberikan Saran)

Should = Sebaiknya / Seharusnya

📌 Pola Kalimat:

You should + Bare Infinitive (Kata Kerja Dasar)
BENAR

You should wash your face.

(Kata kerja dasar)

SALAH

You should washes your face.

(Salah tambahan -es)

Contoh Kalimat Saran 6 Kebiasaan:

  • 1. You should take a bath.
  • 2. You should brush your teeth.
  • 3. You should wash your face.
  • 4. You should wash your hair.
  • 5. You should comb your hair.
  • 6. You should clip your nails.
2

"Asking About Frequency"

Menanyakan Frekuensi Kebiasaan

🔹 Subjek: You / I

Q: How often do you [verb]?

A: I [verb] [frequency].

Contoh:

❓ How often do you brush your teeth?

💬 I brush my teeth twice a day.

🔹 Subjek: She / He (Ketentuan Khusus!)

Q: How often does she/he [verb]?

A: She/He [verb+s/es] [frequency].

Contoh:

❓ How often does she wash her hair?

💬 She washes her hair three times a week.

⚠️ Catatan Tata Bahasa: Penggunaan 'does' untuk subjek She/He dan wajib menambahkan akhiran -s/-es pada kata kerja di kalimat jawaban (misal: wash ➔ washes, brush ➔ brushes).
Portal 3

Frequency & Schedule

Frekuensi & Jadwal Kebersihan

Ungkapan Frekuensi (Frequency Words)

Twice a day Dua kali sehari
Three times a week Tiga kali seminggu
Once a week Satu kali seminggu
Every day Setiap hari
Every morning Setiap pagi
Every night Setiap malam

💡 Penjelasan Perbedaan Kata:

• Once = 1 kali

• Twice = 2 kali

• Three times = 3 kali (dst. four times, five times)

• A day = sehari | • A week = seminggu
